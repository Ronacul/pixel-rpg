Measurements

px = opaque pixels, fill = share of the 16x16 cell, readable = share of the sprite's pixels with at least 2:1 contrast against the darkest-readable biome floor (aim for 40% or more so monsters stand out), look-alike = closest other sprite silhouette (IoU, below 0.75 reads as different), Δ = pixels changed since the saved baseline.
